Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

  • This OAuth client is used only by its owner, through rclone running on the owner's own computer.
  • It requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly) to list and size the owner's own files.
  • Access tokens are stored only in rclone's configuration file on that computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising or AI training.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
